# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

SRE (Site Reliability Engineers), DevOps Leads, Infrastructure Engineers, and CTOs at high-growth tech scaleups and enterprise companies.

## Product Purpose

AstraWatch is a million-dollar enterprise autonomous observability & AI self-healing platform that detects infrastructure anomalies via eBPF kernel probes and automatically remediates root-cause failures in Kubernetes clusters.

## Positioning

Zero-overhead kernel-level eBPF tracing + Isolation Forest ML anomaly detection + automated blast-radius guarded K8s auto-healing operator. Positioned against legacy APMs charging exorbitant per-GB ingestion fees.

## Operating Context

Unauthenticated visitors evaluate the platform on the landing page, marketing sections, interactive sandbox, pricing, and authentication pages (Sign In / Register) before converting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pristine, award-winning 21st.dev / Framer / Linear-grade UI/UX.
- Professional enterprise dark theme with crisp typography, subtle border gradients, cohesive color system, and modern layout rhythm.
- Unauthenticated pages include: Landing Page (`/landing`), Sign In / Register Auth Pages (`/auth/login`, `/auth/register`).

## Brand Commitments

Name: AstraWatch. Professional, authoritative, cutting-edge, high-trust enterprise infrastructure reliability.

## Evidence on Hand

- eBPF kernel tracing (<0.5% CPU overhead, <50µs latency).
- Isolation Forest ML Engine (99.4% precision).
- K8s Auto-Healing Operator (3-second MTTR).
- ClickHouse columnar analytics & live topology graph discovery.

## Product Principles

1. **Enterprise Professionalism Over Gimmicks**: Clean, authoritative, sophisticated design that communicates high-stakes infrastructure reliability.
2. **21st.dev & Framer Design Craft**: Precise micro-interactions, cohesive typography, subtle glowing borders, interactive bento grids, and backdrop glass elements.
3. **Flawless Information Hierarchy**: Clear narrative arc from problem to mechanism, live interactive demonstration, ROI proof, and conversion.
